Smokefree Days: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Smokefree Days is an Android app that helps you track your time without smoking. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

No data collected

The app does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer cannot see anything you enter.

Everything stays on your device

All information you enter is stored only on your phone. The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the device.

Android backup

Android's own backup (Auto Backup) may copy the app's data to your Google account, as it does for most apps. That backup is run by Android and Google, not by the app, and the developer has no access to it. You can turn it on or off in your phone's system settings.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules it on your phone. Notifications are created locally; nothing is sent over a network.

Optional purchase

The app offers an optional one-time purchase. Google Play processes the payment under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The developer receives no payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
